# Curves

A **curve** describes how a movement unfolds over time. The same slide from left to right can start slowly and speed up, arrive with a gentle brake, or bounce at the end. This feeling is set by the **easing**.

## Choosing an easing

Wherever an animation or a transition has an **Easing** setting, pick a ready-made curve from the list (linear, ease in, ease out, ease in-out, and others). Linear moves at a constant speed, which often looks mechanical; ease in-out looks natural for most movements.

## Custom curves

Choose **Custom curve (Bezier)** to draw your own. The Curves editor shows a graph: time runs along the bottom, progress up the side. Drag the two handles to shape the curve. A steep part of the curve is fast, a flat part is slow. You can preview the movement while you adjust it.

## Tips

- Keep the same easing for similar movements so the game feels consistent.
- Short durations (a quarter to half a second) with ease out feel responsive for interface elements.
